# Frequency Balance and Equalization

Treat spectral language as a hypothesis about relationships, not a fixed frequency lookup.

- Diagnose whether “mud,” “harshness,” “air,” or “forwardness” originates in the target, a competing source, dynamics, or space.
- Prefer solving masking at the source that creates it when that preserves the target’s character.
- Use broad tonal moves for balance and narrow moves for stable resonances; validate both in the full mix.
- Audition level-matched bypass because an EQ move can change perceived loudness.
- Protect intelligibility, body, and transient identity through explicit preservation constraints.

For vocals, forwardness may come from level, masking reduction, envelope, early reflections, or automation. Rank these mechanisms from project evidence and user preference rather than stacking them automatically.
